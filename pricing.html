Pricing Questions & Answers

Common questions about ValVoice plans, billing, and access

Can I use ValVoice for free?

Yes! You can download ValVoice directly from our Download page and use core text-to-speech chat conversion. The Free plan includes core OCR and sample agent voices with a weekly message limit.

How does the Pro yearly billing discount work?

The Pro Yearly plan costs $15 per year, which equates to just $1.25 per month. That represents a 58% savings compared to paying the $3 monthly rate on an ongoing basis.

Is the Lifetime plan truly a one-time purchase?

Yes. The Lifetime plan is a single $30 one-time payment. There are no recurring fees or automatic renewals, and you receive permanent access to ValVoice along with future updates and new agent voices.

How will paid commercial checkout work?

ValVoice is currently available for free evaluation while commercial checkout is being integrated. When paid checkout goes live, transactions will be fulfilled safely through Paddle, our authorized Merchant of Record, supporting major credit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, and local currencies.

Can I cancel my subscription at any time?

Yes. If you subscribe to Pro, you can cancel renewal anytime through your Paddle billing portal or by contacting our support team. Your Pro benefits remain active until the end of your prepaid billing period.
