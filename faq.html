Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ValVoice.

01

What is ValVoice?

ValVoice is a Windows application that turns Valorant in-game text messages into spoken voice. Instead of constantly reading the chat, you can listen to messages while playing and stay focused on the game.

02

How does ValVoice convert Valorant text chat into voice?

ValVoice automatically detects messages appearing in your Valorant chat and reads them aloud using AI-powered voices. You can also send the generated voice into your team's voice chat so your teammates can hear it.

03

Does ValVoice work on Windows?

Yes! ValVoice is designed specifically for Windows PCs. Simply install the application, set it up, and use it alongside Valorant while you play.

04

What is Valorant text-to-voice?

Valorant text-to-voice means turning messages typed in the game's chat into spoken audio. With ValVoice, you can listen to chat messages instead of having to read them during a match.

05

Is ValVoice safe to use with Valorant?

ValVoice is designed to work alongside Valorant without modifying its game files or injecting code into the game. It operates as a separate Windows application. However, it is not officially approved or endorsed by Riot Games.

06

How does ValVoice read chats?

ValVoice automatically detects text messages appearing in your Valorant chat and turns them into spoken voice. It works in the background while you play, so you can focus on your game without constantly checking the chat.

07

What is VB-CABLE used for?

VB-CABLE is a free tool that helps ValVoice send its spoken messages into Valorant's voice chat. Think of it as a connection between ValVoice and your game's microphone input. You need to install and configure it to let your teammates hear the generated voices.
